## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They cause no
damage to your computer and contain no viruses. Cookies serve to make a service
more user-friendly, effective and secure.

## Which cookies we use

This website is a purely static site. It currently sets **no cookies** and
embeds no analytics, tracking or advertising services. The fonts used are served
from our own server, so no connections to third parties are established for them.

When you open the site, only the technically necessary access data is recorded in
the server log files of our hosting provider. Which data this is and how long it
is stored is described in the "Server log files" section of our
[privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Controlling cookies in your browser

You can set your browser to inform you about the setting of cookies, to allow
cookies only in individual cases, to exclude the acceptance of cookies for
certain cases or in general, and to activate the automatic deletion of cookies
when you close the browser. If cookies are deactivated, the functionality of this
website may be limited.

## Changes

Should statistics or marketing cookies be used in future, we will obtain your
consent beforehand and update this page accordingly.
